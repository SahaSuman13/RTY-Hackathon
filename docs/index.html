    Where the target ranges come from

    Every target is labelled by strength of evidence. We never invent a number: if research gives only a direction, the range is a coach-adjustable starter value and says so.

    Table tennis framework used

    Ready stance, forehand drive/topspin and backhand drive checks follow our team's Table Tennis Technique & AI Movement Evaluation Framework (11 sections: ready stance, forehand, backhand, topspin, push, chop, block, footwork, checklist, landmarks, cameras), compiled from VICTAS, Table Tennis Teacher, PingSkills, DTTA (German TT Association) and 卓球レポート coaching sources. Extra research: Mao et al. 2023, Applied Sciences (straighter elbow and lower stance vs backspin; advanced players flex the elbow less at the stroke).

    Not yet measurable from body pose alone (framework §13): racket-face angle, exact ball contact, spin. Roadmap: racket and ball detection, a second (front) camera, high-speed video.

    What RallyIQ deliberately does not claim

    • It does not certify a bowling action as legal or illegal (that needs ICC-accredited 3D testing).
    • It does not predict or diagnose injury.
    • A difference from the range is a flag to check with a coach, not an automatic error. Your own best rep is the main benchmark.
    • Joints the model is less than 50% confident about are dropped and shown as "not visible", never guessed.
    • Pose model: Google MediaPipe Pose Landmarker (lite), runs fully in the browser.

    Roadmap

    • Grip check from a close-up hand photo (hand keypoints; fingers are hidden by the bat in full-body video).
    • Higher-accuracy offline analysis (RTMPose / Sports2D) for uploaded match video.
    • Coach-labelled reference reps to replace starter ranges; pro-swing alignment.
    • LLM explanations that receive only flagged numbers and may not add measurements.
